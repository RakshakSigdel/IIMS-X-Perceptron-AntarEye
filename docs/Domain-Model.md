# Domain Model

## Purpose

Defines the core business concepts, ownership, relationships, and business rules.

---

## Domain Overview

```text
Admin
    │
Creates
    │
Doctor
    │
Manages
    │
Patient
    │
Has
    │
Diagnosis Session
    ├── Fundus Image
    ├── AI Prediction
    ├── Heatmap
    ├── Medical Report
    └── Triage Result
```

---

## Business Entities

### Admin

Responsible for platform administration.

Responsibilities

- Create doctor accounts
- Manage doctors
- Maintain the platform

---

### Doctor

Primary user of the system.

Owns:

- Patients
- Diagnosis Sessions

Business Rules

- Can only access their own patients.
- Can only access diagnosis sessions they own.

---

### Patient

Represents a real patient.

Business Rules

- Exists independently of authentication.
- Belongs to exactly one doctor in the MVP.
- Can have multiple diagnosis sessions.

---

### Diagnosis Session

Represents a single patient examination.

Business Rules

- Belongs to one patient.
- Created by one doctor.
- Contains exactly one uploaded fundus image.
- Produces one AI prediction.
- Produces LLM recommendations.
- Produces one heatmap.
- Produces one medical report.
- Has one triage priority.
- Never modified after completion.

---

### Fundus Image

Original retinal image uploaded by the doctor.

Business Rules

- Belongs to one diagnosis session.
- Original image is preserved.

---

### AI Prediction

Structured response returned by the AI service.

Business Rules

- Belongs to one diagnosis session.
- Stores prediction data returned by the AI.
- Never manually edited.

---

### LLM Recommendations

Patient and doctor recommendations generated by the AI service.

Business Rules

- Belong to one diagnosis session.

---

### Heatmap

Visualization generated by the AI.

Business Rules

- Belongs to one diagnosis session.

---

### Medical Report

PDF report generated after diagnosis.

Business Rules

- Belongs to one diagnosis session.
- Represents a snapshot of that diagnosis.

---

### Triage Result

Priority assigned after diagnosis.

Purpose

Helps doctors prioritize patient treatment.

Business Rules

- Belongs to one diagnosis session.
- Derived from AI prediction.

---
